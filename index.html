Privacy Policy

App: Chicago SAFE ZONE Alert Pro
Developer: devsandpros
Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

Chicago SAFE ZONE Alert Pro is a driving-awareness application for Android. It is designed to help drivers stay aware when approaching mapped safety zones in Chicago — including areas near schools, parks, pedestrian crossings, and other high-activity public areas derived from publicly available municipal safety-related data.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what is never done with it.

Chicago SAFE ZONE Alert Pro is an independently developed application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, authorized by, or sponsored by the City of Chicago, the State of Illinois, or any government agency.

2. Location Data

The app uses your device GPS location to provide real-time safety-zone alerts while driving.

Location access is used solely to compare your current position against the app's locally bundled safety-zone database in order to provide audio and visual proximity alerts.

Your location is processed entirely on your device. It is never transmitted to our servers, never stored externally by us, and never shared with third parties.

The app does not use your location for advertising, profiling, analytics, or behavioral tracking.

3. Safety-Zone Database

The safety-zone database used by the app is bundled directly inside the application at the time of installation.

Safety-zone information is derived from publicly available municipal and traffic-safety-related data sources, including publicly accessible information provided by the City of Chicago.

Official public sources may include:

  • https://data.cityofchicago.org/
  • https://www.chicago.gov/

The database may be updated periodically through application updates distributed via the Google Play Store.

4. No Account Required

The app does not require account creation, sign-in, or submission of personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

5. In-App Purchase

The app offers a one-time purchase to unlock the full version.

All payment transactions are securely processed entirely by the Google Play Store. We do not collect, process, or store payment or billing information.

6. No Advertising

The app does not display third-party advertisements and does not use advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers.

7. Diagnostic Data

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics (provided by Google LLC) to automatically collect anonymous crash reports if the application encounters unexpected errors.

Crash reports may include:

  • Device model
  • Android OS version
  • App version
  • Technical crash diagnostics and stack traces

No personally identifiable information is intentionally collected through crash reporting.

Crash diagnostics are used solely to identify and fix software issues.

You can review Google's privacy practices at: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Google Play may additionally collect anonymized crash and performance diagnostics through Android Vitals, a platform-level service provided by Google Play and governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through this application, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.

9. Your Rights

Because the app does not store personal information on our external servers, there is generally no user data maintained by us to access, correct, export, or delete.

Application preferences stored locally on your device may be removed at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing app storage through Android settings.

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be published at the same URL with an updated revision date.

Continued use of the application after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
